The problems Jeanerette homes call about most

Two calls come up more than any others here. One is a slow or blocked drain, and the other is a water heater that has stopped working or begun to leak. Both are tied to the kind of water and the age of the houses in town, and the next sections explain how.

Hard water and the City of Jeanerette system

The City of Jeanerette Water System draws groundwater from three wells and treats it with chloramine. Its 2024 water quality report lists treated water hardness at 287.8 mg/L as calcium carbonate, which works out to roughly 17 grains per gallon. By the usual scale that is very hard water, and it leaves scale on heating elements, inside tank bottoms and in the aerators on faucets.

In practice that means water heaters in Jeanerette often show sediment trouble sooner than a unit in a soft water town. A tank that rumbles, takes longer to heat or runs out of hot water early is a candidate for a flush or an element replacement. See water heater repair for how those fixes go, and ask the city about the current report if you want the latest figures.

Hard water and your heater

A flush once a year clears the loose sediment and slows scale. Skipping it can leave a hard layer that cannot be flushed out.

Tankless units suffer more, because scale narrows the small passages in the heat exchanger. See tankless water heater service if you own one.

Older homes along the Teche

Jeanerette was incorporated as a town in 1878, and its economy grew from cypress lumber and sugar. Louisiana Highways 182 and 87 run along Bayou Teche through town, and older houses along the route can have galvanized supply lines and cast iron drains that corrode from the inside, so drains clog sooner and pressure falls over time.

If you live in an older house and see rusty water, low pressure or repeat clogs, ask about drain cleaning with a camera look, or a repipe walk-through. A plumber can tell you what the pipe actually is before you spend money.

A cold-weather lesson from January 2025

During the January 2025 winter storm, a weather station five miles northwest of Jeanerette recorded a low of 7 degrees. Few homes in town were built for that cold. Pipes in attics and outside walls, hose bibs and water heater supply lines were the usual victims. If a pipe has split, close the main first, then call, and read our page on burst pipe repair.
