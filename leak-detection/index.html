A hidden leak does not announce itself. It shows up as a wet patch that never dries, a warm spot on the floor, the sound of water when everything is off or a bill that jumps with no change in habits. If water is actively running or soaking a wall, shut off the main valve and call the 24/7 line. A same-day appointment is possible when a slot is open.

Leak detection finds the exact point where a pipe is losing water so that a repair can be small and targeted. Instead of opening walls or floors on a guess, the plumber narrows the location with a meter test, listening equipment and, where useful, a thermal camera.

Is water leaking right now: a quick check

1

Turn off every fixture.

Close taps, stop the washer and dishwasher and make sure no one flushes.

2

Read the meter.

Find the meter box near the street and look at the small low-flow dial or leak indicator. If it moves with everything off, water is escaping somewhere.

3

Check the main valve.

Close the main shutoff at the house and read the meter again. If it still moves, the leak is between the meter and the house.

4

Note the signs.

Write down warm floor spots, damp drywall, musty smells and the sound of running water.

Where leaks hide in New Iberia homes

Many homes in south Louisiana sit on concrete slabs, so supply lines can run under the floor where a pinhole leak is invisible for months. If your floor feels warm or you hear water with everything off, see slab leak repair. Older houses in the East Main Street Historic District, built between 1834 and 1933, may also have older pipe that corrodes at joints and fittings.

Outdoors, a leak between the water meter and the house can soak a lawn without breaking the surface. Soft, green or sunken ground near the meter line during a dry spell is a strong sign. Repairs to that stretch are covered under water line repair.

Why finding it first saves money

A plumber who locates the leak to within a few feet can open a small access hole instead of a large one. That means less drywall to patch, fewer tiles to match and a shorter job. Detection also gives your insurance agent a clear record of where and why water escaped.

What happens after the leak is found

1

Confirm.

The meter test shows there is a leak and the plumber rules out toilets and irrigation.

2

Pinpoint.

Listening gear and thermal imaging narrow the location, and the plumber marks it.

3

Explain.

You hear what the options are, from a small spot repair to rerouting a line, and the price for each.

4

Repair.

With your approval the leak is repaired, the pressure is retested and you are told what to watch for.

Take it seriously

Even a small leak can wet insulation, feed mold and soften framing over months. If you see a ceiling stain growing or smell mustiness, act within days. For a pipe that has burst and is flooding now, go straight to burst pipe repair.
