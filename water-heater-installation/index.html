A planned water heater install is the calm version of an emergency swap. You pick the unit, set the date and the plumber arrives ready to connect, vent and test it. Call during the day to schedule, and same-day appointments are possible when the schedule allows.

Water heater installation covers new construction, additions, a move from the garage to a closet, a switch from electric to gas and an upgrade to a larger or more efficient unit. In each case the work is more than setting a tank in place. The vent, the gas line or circuit, the relief valve and the drain pan all have to match the unit.

Planning a new install

Questions to settle before the visit

  • How many people use hot water, and how many bathrooms does the house have?
  • Is there a gas line near the location, or does the unit need a dedicated electrical circuit?
  • Where does the vent run, and is there fresh air for a gas burner?
  • Is the floor strong and level, and is there a drain or pan to catch a leak?
  • Do you want to place the heater above the floor to keep it out of rising water?

Gas or electric

Comparing fuel types
Factor.Gas.Electric.
Hot water recovery.Faster, because the burner heats quickly.Slower for the same tank size.
Venting.Needs a flue and combustion air.No vent needed.
Installation needs.Gas line and flue.Dedicated circuit and breaker.
Best for.Larger families and heavy use.Homes with no gas service.

Which one fits is partly a matter of what your home already has. Changing from one fuel to the other adds work, such as a new gas line or an electrical upgrade, so the plumber looks at the space before pricing the job.

Where to put it in a Louisiana home

Many homes in the New Iberia area have slab foundations, so the heater sits in a garage, a utility closet or the attic. Attic installs need a drain pan piped to an outlet, because a leak above a ceiling can do more damage than one on a garage floor. In garages, a raised platform and protection from vehicle impact are standard concerns.

How the install goes

Permits and licensing

The City of New Iberia Permit and Inspection Department says a permit must be obtained for any and all work, including plumbing. Confirm what applies at your address by calling (337) 369-2354. Plumbers in Louisiana are licensed through the State Plumbing Board of Louisiana, which lists its site as spbla.com.

If you are replacing a failed unit, see water heater replacement. For on-demand heating, the tankless water heater page explains the extra requirements.

Sources behind this page

Venting, relief valves and gas connections must meet the Louisiana State Uniform Construction Code, according to the Louisiana Department of Health, and the manufacturer's instructions. Ask which standards apply to your location.

What affects the price of a water heater installation

Installs are quoted before work starts. These are the factors that decide the number.

Unit size and efficiency.Larger or higher efficiency tanks cost more.
Fuel and venting.A new vent or gas line adds work.
Electrical work.A new circuit or breaker panel work changes the scope.
Access and location.Attic installs take longer than garage installs.
Required extras.Pan, drain line, expansion tank and shutoffs add to the job.

Planned upgrades are normally paid out of pocket. Some utilities and manufacturers offer rebates on efficient models, so ask your utility before you buy.
