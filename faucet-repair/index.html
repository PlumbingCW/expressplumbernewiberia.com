A faucet that will not shut off or sprays water can flood a room while you look for the valve. Close the shutoff beneath the sink, or the main valve at the house if there is none, and call the 24/7 line. Most faucet repairs are quick once the plumber has the right parts.

Faucet repair covers drips, low flow, handles that loosen or stick, spouts that leak at the base and fixtures that have failed entirely. Many faucets can be repaired with a new cartridge or seat, and a plumber can tell you whether the faucet is worth keeping.

Faucet failure: first steps

1

Find the shutoff.

Look beneath the sink for two small valves. Turn them clockwise until they stop.

2

If there is no valve.

Close the main valve at the house or the meter.

3

Catch the drip.

A towel and bucket under the sink protect the cabinet floor.

4

Note the brand.

Look for a name on the handle or base. It helps the plumber bring the right cartridge.

Why faucets fail

A steady drip from the spout.

A worn cartridge, washer or valve seat.

Water around the handle.

A failed O-ring or packing nut.

Low pressure at one faucet.

A clogged aerator, or mineral buildup in the cartridge.

Handle that is stiff or loose.

A worn cartridge or a stripped set screw.

Repair or replace

A quick guide

The effect of hard water on faucets

Mineral scale clogs aerators and wears cartridges. The hardness of the water supplied in New Iberia is a figure your water utility can report. If your aerators scale up quickly, a plumber can recommend a descaling routine or a different cartridge type.

How a faucet repair goes

1

Shut off and inspect.

Water is closed at the fixture, and the faucet is disassembled to see the failed part.

2

Match the parts.

The cartridge, seat or O-ring is matched to the faucet brand and model.

3

Rebuild or replace.

The faucet is rebuilt, or a new one is installed if the old one is beyond saving.

4

Test.

Hot, cold, flow and the supply connections are checked for leaks.

Showers and tubs

Tub and shower valves hide behind the wall, so a dripping tub spout may need a cartridge replaced from the front or an access panel opened behind it. If a drip is staining the ceiling below, ask about leak detection. For a fixture that is being replaced as part of a remodel, see plumbing installation.

Sources behind this page

Cartridge and part matches follow the faucet maker's specification, and a plumber reads the model number before ordering. Plumbers are licensed by the State Plumbing Board of Louisiana, according to spbla.com.

Safety: when to call right away

If a faucet is spraying and you cannot stop it, close the main valve and call. Do not force a seized valve with a wrench.
