Your emergency plumber in New Iberia, LA, open 24/7

When water is coming through a ceiling or sewage is rising in a tub, you need an emergency plumber who picks up the phone. Our 24/7 line connects you with a dispatcher who asks what is happening, talks you through shutting off water if needed and books the next open same-day appointment. No arrival time is promised, because it depends on the schedule.

We handle the emergencies that cannot wait: burst and frozen pipes, sewer backups, overflowing toilets, leaking water heaters and failed water lines. We also handle planned work such as water heater installs, remodel plumbing and repiping, and we explain the price before any job begins.

What counts as a plumbing emergency

Call the 24/7 line right away if

  • Water is flowing from a broken pipe and you cannot stop it.
  • Sewage is backing up into a tub, shower or floor drain.
  • A water heater tank is leaking onto the floor.
  • A toilet is overflowing and will not stop.
  • You smell gas near a gas water heater. Leave the house first and call your gas utility from outside.

Not sure whether it qualifies? Call and describe it. The dispatcher can tell you whether it can wait for a morning appointment or needs the next open slot tonight.

Plumbing in a town with real history

New Iberia was founded in 1779, and the East Main Street Historic District lists buildings dating from 1834 to 1933. Houses of that age often hide older drain and supply pipe, and a plumber who looks at the pipe itself gives better advice than one who guesses from the address. Newer neighborhoods along Admiral Doyle Drive and U.S. 90 have different plumbing and different problems.

Storms shape the work here too. Hurricane Ike flooded parts of the city in September 2008, and the January 2025 freeze set an all-time low of 2 degrees at the New Iberia airport station. Plumbing that sat in floodwater or froze needs a careful look before it is used again.

Standards and licensing

Plumbers in Louisiana are licensed by the State Plumbing Board of Louisiana, according to its website at spbla.com, and plumbing requirements are published as part of the Louisiana State Uniform Construction Code. We follow those standards, and the City of New Iberia says a permit must be obtained for any and all work, including plumbing.

Safety: when to call right away

If you smell gas, leave the building before you do anything else. Do not use switches or phones inside.
