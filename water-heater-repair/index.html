A water heater that has stopped heating is an inconvenience. One that is leaking is an emergency. If water is spreading from the base of the tank, shut off the cold water supply valve above the heater, switch off the power or set the gas control to pilot, and call the 24/7 line. A same-day appointment is possible when a slot is open.

Water heater repair covers the parts that fail before the tank does: heating elements, thermostats, gas valves, thermocouples, igniters, anode rods, relief valves and dip tubes. The plumber finds which part failed, tells you whether replacing it makes sense for the age of your unit and gives you the price before work begins.

Leaking water heater: what to do first

1

Close the cold supply.

The shutoff valve sits on the pipe running into the top of the tank. Turn it clockwise until it stops.

2

Cut the power or gas.

Flip the breaker for an electric unit. On a gas unit, turn the control knob to pilot or off.

3

Find where the water comes from.

A drip at a pipe fitting is a repair. Water coming from the tank body itself usually means the tank has failed.

4

Protect the floor.

Move boxes off the floor and put towels or a pan under the heater until the plumber arrives.

Repair or replace

A tank that is leaking from its shell cannot be repaired. Most tank heaters last about ten years, and a unit near that age with a failed part may cost more to fix than its remaining life is worth. The plumber reads the manufacture date on the rating label, explains both options and gives a price for each, so you decide with the numbers in front of you. If replacement is the better call, see water heater replacement.

Local conditions that shorten heater life

South Louisiana heat and humidity affect heaters in garages and attic closets, where combustion air, rusted pans and corroded fittings are common. Flooding is the other risk. After Hurricane Ike in September 2008 put parts of New Iberia under water, any heater with a controls section that sat in floodwater needed to be checked before it was powered again. A unit that has been submerged should not be switched back on until a plumber has looked at it.

Mineral content is the quiet cause of sediment, and the hardness of New Iberia's water is a number your water utility can give you. Ask them for the current hardness. That figure tells you how fast scale is likely to form and how often a flush makes sense.

Safety checks that matter on gas units

A gas water heater needs a clear vent, combustion air and a steady flame. If you smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility from outside before you call us. A yellow, lazy flame, soot at the draft hood or a scorched wall behind the unit are reasons to have the heater checked promptly.

Sources behind this page

Water heater work follows the Louisiana State Uniform Construction Code, according to the Louisiana Department of Health. The manufacturer's installation manual also sets requirements for venting, relief valves and clearances, and the plumber follows both.

What affects the price of water heater repair
