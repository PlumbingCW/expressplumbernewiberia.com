Privacy Policy

What we collect, why and how to ask questions.

Call Now

This policy explains how New Iberia Express Plumbing handles information when you use this website or call us. Effective date: November 14, 2026.

Information we collect

This site does not use contact forms or accounts. If you call, we may collect your name, phone number, address and a description of the problem so we can schedule a visit. If you email us, we collect the email address and the message you send. Calls may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

How we use it

We use the information to schedule and carry out plumbing work, answer questions, improve our service and meet legal duties. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set advertising cookies of its own. Your browser may load fonts from Google Fonts, which can receive your IP address. Maps embedded on the site are provided by Google and are subject to Google's privacy policy.

Sharing

We share information only with the plumbers and staff who need it to serve you, with service providers who help us run the business and when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete information we hold about you. Email contact@expressplumbernewiberia.com or call (855) 575-2890. We may need to keep some records, such as work performed.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the effective date when we do.
