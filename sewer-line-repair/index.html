A cracked or collapsed sewer line is an emergency when sewage backs into the house or the yard sinks. Call the 24/7 line, stop using water and keep the cleanout area clear so the plumber can reach the line. A same-day appointment is possible when a slot is open, and the first job is usually to clear the line and find the break with a camera.

Sewer line repair fixes the physical damage in the pipe that carries waste from your house to the public sewer or a septic tank. Depending on what the camera shows, that means a spot repair at one break, a liner through a long damaged run or a full replacement of the line.

Sewer line failure: signs to act on today

Backups that return after cleaning.

A line that clogs every few months has a defect holding the blockage in place.

A wet or sunken patch in the yard.

Soil that stays soggy without rain can mean a break leaking wastewater.

Slow drains and gurgling together.

A partly collapsed section restricts flow and pushes air back up the vents.

Sewer smell outdoors.

Gas escaping near the foundation or a cracked cleanout cap.

Your repair options

Spot repair, lining and replacement
Option.Best when.Trade-off.
Spot repair.One break or one bad joint on an otherwise sound line.Needs a dig at the break, so the yard or slab is opened at that point.
Pipe lining.A long run with cracks, roots or pinholes but a shape that is still round.Reduces the pipe diameter slightly and will not fix a collapse.
Replacement.A collapsed, offset or badly sagging line.Highest cost and the most disruption, but a fresh line.

The right answer depends on the camera footage. A plumber who recommends replacement without showing you the video has skipped a step. Ask to watch the footage, and ask for the depth and location of any break so you know how much digging is involved.

Cast iron and older clay lines

Older homes can have cast iron drain pipe that rusts through at the bottom of the pipe or clay lines that shift at the joints. Cast iron can often be repaired with a section replaced or a sleeve at the damaged spot. The East Main Street Historic District has buildings dating from 1834 to 1933, and houses of that age are the ones most likely to need a close look at the original drain materials.

Permits, licensing and who may do the work

The City of New Iberia Permit and Inspection Department states that a permit must be obtained for any and all work, including electrical, plumbing and mechanical. Ask it at (337) 369-2354 what applies to a sewer repair at your address, because the answer can depend on whether the property is inside the city limits.

In Louisiana, plumbers are licensed by the State Plumbing Board of Louisiana, which lists its website as spbla.com. You can check any plumber's license there before work begins, including ours. Plumbing requirements are published as part of the Louisiana State Uniform Construction Code, according to the Louisiana Department of Health.

What affects the price of sewer line repair

Repair prices vary more than almost any other plumbing job. A quote follows the camera work.

Depth of the break.A shallow break is a smaller dig than one under a driveway.
Length of damage.A single joint costs less than a long run of cracked pipe.
Surface above the pipe.Lawn, concrete and landscaping change the restoration work.
Pipe material and size.Cast iron, clay and PVC are repaired differently.
Permits and inspection.Where a permit is needed, it affects timing.

A sewer break is often a maintenance issue rather than a covered loss. Policies differ on what they cover for sudden damage, so read yours or ask your agent.

Safety: when to call right away

Sewage can carry bacteria. Keep children and pets away from the wet area and wash hands well after any contact. Do not run water while the line is backed up.
