Garbage Disposal Repair Cost in New Iberia, LA: What Changes the Price

A reset costs little and a replacement costs more. Here is what decides which one you need and what shapes the final price.

Plumber on call.
(855) 575-2890
Call Now

People ask what a garbage disposal repair costs before they ask what is wrong with it. The honest answer is that it depends on what failed, and that is something a plumber can usually tell in a few minutes. This guide explains what moves the price so you can judge a quote when you get one.

We do not list fixed prices here because the range is wide. A reset or a jam clear is a small job. A new unit with a new electrical connection is a bigger one. What follows covers the decision points.

Repair or replace: the first question

Many disposals last roughly eight to twelve years. A unit near that age with a failed motor or a cracked housing is usually cheaper to replace than to repair. A younger unit with a loose connection is a quick fix.

What decides the price

Horsepower.

A half horsepower disposal suits light use. Three quarters to one horsepower suits a busy kitchen and costs more.

Electrical connection.

A plug-in unit is simple. A hardwired one, or a sink with no nearby outlet, adds work.

Plumbing changes.

A new trap, dishwasher hose or air gap adds parts and time.

Sink condition.

A cracked or loose flange may need a new mounting kit or sink repair.

What causes disposals to fail early

Grease, fibrous scraps and bones are the usual culprits. Rice, pasta and coffee grounds swell or pack in the drain below the disposal. Local cooking has its own challenges, from boiled crawfish shells to rice dishes. Shells and bones belong in the trash. A disposal is for soft scraps and small amounts.

Before you call: safe checks

1

Switch off and unplug.

Turn off the wall switch. Unplug the unit if it plugs in under the sink.

2

Hands out.

Never reach into the disposal. Use tongs to remove anything you can see.

3

Try the hex key.

Insert it in the socket under the unit and turn it back and forth to free the blades.

4

Press reset.

Wait a few minutes after a trip, then press the red button on the bottom.

If those steps do not work, the plumber can diagnose the rest. See garbage disposal repair for the full process. If the sink backs up even with the disposal off, the blockage is further down and drain cleaning is the right call.

Questions to ask when you get a quote

Make the quote easy to compare

  • Does the price include the diagnosis, or is that a separate charge?
  • If a new unit is needed, what horsepower and brand is it, and what is the warranty?
  • Is wiring or a new outlet included if the sink has no power nearby?
  • Will the old unit be hauled away?
  • Is there a charge if you decide not to go ahead after the diagnosis?

A quote that spells those out is easier to trust than one that gives only a total. Ask for the price in writing before the work starts. A plumber who has found the cause will usually explain it in plain words, and you should feel free to ask what happens if the disposal is left alone.

Keeping the next disposal alive

A few habits stretch the life of a new unit. Run cold water before, during and after each use, so fats stay solid and move down the line. Grind small amounts at a time and keep stringy vegetables, bones and shells out. Never put grease down the drain, even with the disposal running, because it cools below the unit and coats the pipe.
