Sewage in a tub or shower drain is a stop-everything problem. Our emergency line is open 24/7, and a plumber can be scheduled the same day when a slot is open. Until help arrives, stop all water use, keep children and pets out of the wet area and do not run the washer or dishwasher.

Sewer line cleaning clears the main drain that carries every fixture's waste out of the house toward the street or a septic system. It uses a long cable machine or a water jet, and it ends with a flow test and, when the problem repeats, a camera look at what is inside the line.

Sewer backup: what to do first

1

Stop all water use.

Flushing and draining add to what is already coming back up. Wait for the line to be cleared.

2

Avoid contact.

Sewage carries bacteria. Wear boots and gloves if you must enter the room, and wash hands well afterward.

3

Check the cleanout.

A capped cleanout near the house or in the yard gives the plumber direct access. Clear brush and mulch away from it.

4

Call the right office.

If the street or several neighbors are affected, the city wastewater department may be dealing with the public main. Otherwise the line is yours.

Public main or private line

The City of New Iberia Wastewater Department serves more than 33,000 city users and about 1,500 parish users through two treatment plants and 75 pump stations. Its page lists sewer emergency numbers: (337) 369-2347 on weekdays until 4:30 pm and (337) 373-3111 after hours, on weekends and on holidays.

Outside the city limits, sewer service may come from Iberia Parish Sewerage District No. 1, which the city page lists at (337) 369-2376. Your sewer bill names your provider. If a camera shows the blockage on your side of the property line, that part is a plumbing job, and that is where we come in.

Why main lines back up

Roots through a joint.

Fine roots enter at a joint and catch paper until the line stops.

Grease and wipes.

Products labeled flushable still collect at bends and at the cleanout.

A sagging section.

A low spot holds water and solids, and every flush leaves some behind.

A broken or offset pipe.

Soil enters the line and builds a dam that a cable keeps tearing open.

After heavy rain and storms

Hurricane Ike in September 2008 flooded parts of New Iberia when the lakes overflowed, and a December 2022 tornado damaged the southeastern part of the city. Storms like these leave silt, debris and stressed pipe behind. If sewer backups follow a big rain, ask for a camera pass once the line is clear to see whether stormwater or soil is entering the pipe.

Cleaning methods, step by step

1

Find the cleanout.

The plumber opens the cleanout and checks whether water stands in the line.

2

Cable the blockage.

A cable machine with a cutting head clears paper, grease and roots at the stoppage.

3

Jet if needed.

Hydro jetting follows when the wall is coated, as described on the hydro jetting page.

4

Camera and report.

A short sewer inspection shows the cause so you know whether it will come back.

Septic systems

Some homes outside town run on septic tanks. Plumbers clear the pipes that lead to a tank, but tank pumping is a separate trade. If your drains are slow and you have a septic system, say so on the call, because the cause may be a full tank rather than a clogged line.

If a broken pipe is the cause, sewer line repair follows the cleaning. For single fixtures, drain cleaning is the lighter job.

What affects the price of sewer line cleaning

You get a price for the cleaning before work starts. These factors shape it.

Length of the line.A long run from the house to the street needs more cable.
What is blocking it.Paper clears quickly. Roots and hardened grease take longer.
Cleaning method.A cable is the starting point and a jetter takes more equipment.
Camera follow-up.A video record adds a step and helps with a repair plan.
