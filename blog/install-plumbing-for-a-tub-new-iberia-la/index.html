How to Install Plumbing for a Tub: A New Iberia, LA Homeowner Guide

A tub needs a drain, an overflow, a vent and hot and cold supply. On a slab, the drain is the hard part.

Plumber on call.
(855) 575-2890
Call Now

Installing the plumbing for a tub is more than hooking up a faucet. A tub needs a drain with a trap, a vent so it drains smoothly, a valve and a spout, and hot and cold supply lines. In many south Louisiana homes the floor is a slab, which changes how the drain is run.

This guide walks through the pieces and the order the work happens in. It does not replace a plumber's site visit, but it helps you plan.

The parts of a tub plumbing job

1

Rough-in drain and trap.

The drain line runs from the tub location to the main drain, with a trap to hold water that blocks sewer gas.

2

Vent.

A vent line lets air into the drain so water flows freely and the trap does not siphon dry.

3

Supply lines and valve.

Hot and cold lines run to the valve body in the wall, set to the correct depth.

4

Trim and test.

After the tub is set and walls are finished, the spout, handle and overflow are attached and tested.

Slab houses and where the drain goes

If the tub is on a slab, the drain either goes under the slab or the tub sits on a raised platform. Running a drain under a slab means cutting concrete, laying pipe at the right slope and patching. Placing the new tub next to an existing bathroom keeps the new drain short and the cost down.

A camera check of the existing drain with a sewer inspection tells you whether the line can take another fixture. Older drain pipe may be a different material than modern fittings, so the plumber checks first.

Questions to settle before the walls open

Plan these early

  • Where is the nearest drain line and vent, and what are they made of?
  • Will the tub's weight and water need extra floor support?
  • Does the water heater have enough capacity for a large tub?
  • Is there an access panel behind the valve for future repairs?
  • Who is pulling the permit?

Large tubs and hot water

A large soaking tub can use more hot water than a standard unit holds. If your tank is small, you may run cold before the tub is full. The plumber can look at the heater when planning. See plumbing installation for how a remodel visit runs.

Choosing the tub and the valve together

The tub, the valve and the trim should be chosen as a set before the walls close. A freestanding tub needs a floor-mounted filler or a wall spout and has its own drain geometry. An alcove tub with a shower needs a valve that handles both the spout and the showerhead. If you pick the trim after the rough-in, you may find the valve does not fit it.

Order the tub and the valve early, and give the plumber the maker's rough-in sheet. It shows exactly where the drain, the overflow and the valve body must sit.

Testing before the walls close

1

Pressure test the supply.

The new lines are capped and held under pressure to check for leaks at every joint.

2

Fill-test the drain.

The tub is filled and drained while the plumber watches every connection and the trap.

3

Photograph the rough-in.

Pictures of pipe locations inside the wall help with future repairs.

4

Close the walls.

Only after the tests pass does the drywall or tile go up.

Keeping access for the future

Valves and traps fail eventually, and a tub with no access panel means cutting a wall or tile to reach them. Ask the plumber and the contractor to plan an access panel behind the valve, usually in the adjoining closet or room. It costs little during the build and saves a good deal of demolition when a cartridge needs to be replaced years later.

Permit and licensing points for a bathroom project in New Iberia

The City of New Iberia Permit and Inspection Department states that a permit must be obtained for any and all work, including plumbing. Call (337) 369-2354 before you start so that you know what applies, especially if your address is outside city limits.

The State Plumbing Board of Louisiana licenses plumbers and lists its website as spbla.com. Verify a plumber's license there before you sign a contract.
