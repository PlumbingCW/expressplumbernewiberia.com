A tank that has rusted through cannot be patched. If water is pooling under the heater, close the cold supply valve, switch off power or gas and call the 24/7 line. Replacement can often be done on the day you call when a slot is open and the right size unit is on hand.

Water heater replacement means removing the failed tank, installing a new one that fits your household and bringing the connections, venting, relief valve and drain pan up to current requirements. A good replacement visit ends with hot water, a clean install and an explanation of what was changed.

Signs the heater is past repair

Water from the tank shell.

A leak through the tank wall is a failed inner tank. No part can fix it.

Rusty hot water.

Corrosion inside the tank flakes into the water, and it gets worse over time.

Age near ten years.

Many tank heaters fail between eight and twelve years, so a unit at that age is a candidate.

Repeated repairs.

When the second or third part fails within a year, the repair bills add up.

Tank size follows household use. A couple can do well on a 40 gallon tank, while a family with several bathrooms often needs 50 or more. The plumber looks at how many people live in the house, how many bathrooms you have and how you use hot water before recommending a size.

Flood-aware placement

Parts of New Iberia flooded when Hurricane Ike pushed lakes over their banks in September 2008, and low garages across south Louisiana have taken water since. A heater installed on a raised platform keeps burners and controls higher off the floor. Ask whether raising the unit is practical in your space.

If you smell gas near a gas water heater, leave the house first and call your gas utility from outside. Do not relight the pilot.
