A burst pipe floods a house quickly. Shut off the main water valve first, then flip the breaker for any wet outlets if you can reach the panel safely, then call the 24/7 line. A same-day appointment is possible when a slot is open, and the dispatcher can talk you through shutoff while you wait.

Burst pipe repair means stopping the flow, cutting out the damaged section and replacing it with new pipe, then testing the line under pressure. The plumber also checks nearby pipe for damage from the same cause, because a pipe that froze in one spot may be cracked in another.

Burst pipe: what to do in the first ten minutes

1

Shut off the main.

The valve is usually at the meter or where the line enters the house. Turn it fully clockwise.

2

Open a low faucet.

Draining the system relieves pressure and helps water leave the broken section.

3

Protect people and power.

Stay out of standing water near outlets. Cut the breaker only if the panel is dry and you can reach it safely.

4

Document the damage.

Take photos and short videos before moving anything. They help with an insurance claim.

Why pipes burst in south Louisiana

Freezes are rare here, which is exactly why they hurt. When a hard freeze reached south Louisiana during the January 2025 winter storm, many homes had pipes in attics, outside walls and exposed exterior fixtures that were never insulated for it. Water that freezes expands and splits copper, PEX and PVC, and the leak shows up when the pipe thaws.

Not every burst is cold related. Corroded copper, a failed fitting, a pressure spike or a loose supply line can all break at any time of year. The plumber looks at the failure itself and at the pipe around it to tell which cause you have.

The system is turned back on slowly and tested for leaks before the plumber leaves.

Water damage cleanup

Plumbers stop the water and repair the pipe. Drying walls and floors is a separate job done by a water restoration company, and the speed matters because wet drywall and insulation can grow mold within days. Ask your insurance agent which restoration companies they work with.

If the pipe that burst is the line between the meter and the house, see water line repair. For a hidden leak that has not burst yet, leak detection finds it before it fails.
