A drain that has stopped moving water becomes an emergency the moment it threatens a floor. If a tub is filling with gray water, a toilet will not flush or the kitchen sink is gurgling, call the 24/7 line and ask for the next open same-day slot. While you wait, stop running water, keep everyone off the affected fixtures and move rugs and boxes away from floor drains.

Drain cleaning means clearing a blocked line with a hand auger, a motorized cable or a water jet, then testing the line until water leaves every fixture at normal speed. The goal is to find where the blockage sits, not just to poke a hole through it, because a clog that returns in two weeks usually points to something deeper in the pipe.

Drain backup: what to do before the plumber arrives

1

Stop adding water.

Pause the washer, dishwasher and showers. Every gallon you add has nowhere to go and ends up on the floor.

2

Skip the liquid openers.

Chemical openers can burn skin and eyes when a plumber later opens the line. If you already poured some in, say so on the call.

3

Protect the floor.

Lift rugs, move boxes and set towels at the base of the affected fixtures. Floor drains in garages and laundry rooms back up first when the main line is blocked.

4

Note the pattern.

Write down which fixtures are slow or gurgling. One clogged sink points to a branch line, while a tub and toilet backing up together point to the main.

Where clogs start in New Iberia homes

The East Main Street Historic District alone lists 71 buildings on 30 acres, with construction dates running from 1834 to 1933. Houses of that age, and many mid-century homes across Iberia Parish, can have older drain materials with rough inner walls that catch grease and scale. If your home is that old, a camera look after a stubborn clog is worth the extra step.

Newer subdivisions have a different pattern. Smooth plastic pipe clears easily, but wipes, hair and kitchen grease still collect at the first bend below a fixture. Either way the cause shapes the fix, which is why the plumber asks what went down the drain and when the slowdown began.

Not every blockage is yours to fix. The City of New Iberia Wastewater Department runs 120 miles of sewer line, 2,200 manholes and 75 pump stations. If sewage is surfacing in the street or several neighbors are backing up at once, the department lists sewer emergency numbers at (337) 369-2347 on weekdays and (337) 373-3111 after 4:30 pm, on weekends and on holidays. If the trouble stays inside your property line, call us.

Signs a drain needs more than a plunger

Gurgling after a flush.

Air is fighting past a blockage, often in the main line or a vent.

Two or more fixtures slow.

A shared branch or the main is restricted, so one fixture is rarely the cause.

Smell from a floor drain.

Standing water or buildup in the line is releasing sewer gas into the room.

Water in the tub when the washer drains.

The lowest fixture is showing you where the line is blocked.

How a drain gets cleared

1

Locate.

The plumber finds the access point, such as a cleanout, a trap or the fixture itself, and checks which drains are affected.

2

Clear.

A hand auger or motorized cable with a cutting head works through the blockage. Heavy grease may call for hydro jetting.

3

Test.

Water runs through every connected fixture while the plumber watches the flow and listens for gurgling.

4

Explain.

You hear what was found, whether it is likely to return and whether a camera inspection makes sense.

Kitchen sink clogs

Kitchen sinks account for a large share of drain calls because cooking grease cools and hardens a few feet below the strainer. If the sink backs up into the second basin, or the dishwasher air gap spits water, the blockage usually sits past the trap. A disposal that hums but will not turn is a separate problem handled under garbage disposal repair.

When it is a leak, not a clog

A drain that clears but leaves a damp cabinet, a stained ceiling or a musty crawlspace may be leaking. Drain lines leak only while water runs through them, so a pressure test on the supply side can find nothing. A fill-and-watch test or a camera points to the bad joint, and leak detection covers the stains that follow.
