About New Iberia Express Plumbing

A 24/7 plumbing service based in New Iberia, Louisiana, for homes and businesses in town and the nearby towns.

24/7 emergency line.

New Iberia Express Plumbing answers plumbing calls around the clock from 900 E Admiral Doyle Dr in New Iberia. We handle emergencies such as burst pipes, sewer backups and failed water heaters, and planned work such as installs and repiping. Our aim is simple: get a licensed plumber to the problem, explain what is wrong and give you the price before work starts.

How a call works

1

You call.

A dispatcher answers at any hour and asks what is happening and where.

2

We schedule.

You are offered the next open same-day appointment. We do not promise an arrival time.

3

A plumber diagnoses.

The cause is found with the right tools, whether that is a camera, a meter test or listening gear.

4

You decide.

The fix and the price are explained first, and work starts only when you say so.

Licensing and how to check it

Plumbers in Louisiana are licensed by the State Plumbing Board of Louisiana, which lists its website as spbla.com. We encourage you to check any plumber's license there before you hire, including ours. We use the phrase licensed and insured, and we do not publish license numbers on these pages.

What we work on

Our work falls into four groups. Drain and sewer work covers drain cleaning, hydro jetting, sewer line cleaning, sewer line repair and camera inspection. Water heater work covers repair, replacement, installation and tankless units. Leak and pipe work covers leak detection, slab leaks, burst pipes, water lines and repiping. Fixture work covers toilets, faucets and disposals.

We also do plumbing installation for remodels and additions, and commercial plumbing for shops, restaurants and offices. If something is outside what we do, the dispatcher will say so and point you in a useful direction rather than guess.

What you can expect from every visit

Our standards

  • The plumber explains what failed and why before talking about price.
  • You hear the price before work starts and decide whether to go ahead.
  • Permits are handled where the City of New Iberia or your parish requires them.
  • The work is tested before the plumber leaves.
  • You are told what to watch for afterward, and what to do if it returns.

Why the 24/7 line matters

Plumbing problems do not keep office hours. Pipes burst in cold snaps, water heaters give out on holiday weekends and sewers back up when guests are in the house. Answering the phone at any hour is the core of what we do, and it is why the line is staffed around the clock. The dispatcher can also help you shut off water while you wait, which often limits the damage.

We keep the promise modest. We do not promise an arrival time, because jobs and traffic vary. We do promise to answer, to offer the next open same-day appointment and to tell you the price first.

Homes and businesses we serve

Most of our calls come from homeowners, from the older houses near the East Main Street Historic District to newer subdivisions on the edges of town. We also serve restaurants, shops, offices and rental properties along Admiral Doyle Drive and in the downtown blocks near Bayou Teche. Landlords and property managers can ask for a written summary of the work for their records.

Whatever the building, the approach is the same. We ask what is happening, find the cause and explain the choices. If a repair makes sense, we do it. If a bigger job such as a repipe is the better long-term answer, we say so, and you decide on your own schedule.

Before you hire any plumber

A few questions protect you with any contractor. Ask whether the plumber is licensed, and check at spbla.com. Ask for the price in writing before work starts. Ask whether a permit is needed and who will pull it. Ask what the warranty covers and for how long. A plumber who answers these plainly is one worth hiring, and we are glad to answer them on the phone.

A town we know

New Iberia was founded in 1779 on Bayou Teche. The East Main Street Historic District lists 71 buildings on 30 acres, with dates from 1834 to 1933, and Admiral Doyle Drive is the main commercial corridor. Older and newer parts of town have different plumbing, and we plan each job around what the pipe really is.

We also serve the nearby towns of Jeanerette, Loreauville, Delcambre and St. Martinville. See our full list of services for what we do.
